DolandırıcıSavar

A free app that catches fake delivery, bank, government and "police" messages and calls, and warns you.

What it does

  • Warns you about suspicious SMS and calls
  • Checks a suspicious link, message, QR code or screenshot
  • Family protection: tells you when a warning appears on a relative’s phone
  • Works in 10 languages

Privacy

Your messages and calls are checked only on your phone; they are never sent to a server. Details: Privacy policy
