What it does

Scam text alerts

Recognises patterns such as "your parcel could not be delivered", "your account is locked" or "hi mum, this is my new number" and warns you with a notification. No SMS permission needed.

Suspicious call alerts

Warns about unknown international and hidden numbers; can silence or reject reported scam numbers.

Link, QR and screenshot check

Paste a suspicious link, scan a QR code or pick a screenshot of the message; it shows look-alike addresses and scam patterns.

Family protection

Get told when a warning appears on a parent's or relative's phone. Only the type of warning is shared.

Phone security check

Finds apps that can read your screen or give remote access, and sums up screen lock and update status in one score.

Scam guide

Premium

Core protection is always free. Premium is a one-time payment, not a subscription:

  • No ads

  • Protection in WhatsApp, Telegram and other messaging apps
  • Weekly protection summary and 30-day protection report

Privacy

The content of your messages and calls is checked only on your phone; it is never sent to a server or stored. The app has no account and no analytics. Full privacy policy

Deleting your data

  • Alert history: in the app, Alert history → Clear history. Records exist only on your phone and are deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Family protection: remove the link on the Family protection screen; the pairing and event records on the server are deleted. Event records are deleted after 7 days anyway.
  • Numbers you reported: deleted automatically after 90 days.
  • Uninstalling the app: deletes all app data on your phone.

For any other deletion request, write to antiscam.destek@gmail.com; we reply within 30 days at the latest.
