2. Summary

DolandırıcıSavar protects you against SMS, call and link scams while processing your message and call content only on your phone:

  • No account is required. Your messages, the content of your calls, your app list and your alert history are never sent to us.
  • Our server is used only for two optional features: number reports and family protection (see the sections below). If you do not use them, no personal data is sent to our server; the app only downloads your country's list of reported numbers (the request carries nothing but the country calling code).
  • The app is free and shows non-personalised ads (Google AdMob; see §4). Your messages, calls, app list and alert history are never sent to the ad network.
  • The app contains no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • Message texts are never stored.

4. Internet and third parties

The app goes online only when you tap "Follow redirects" for a link: a request is sent to that link without downloading the page or sending cookies. The link owner (possibly a scammer) may see your IP address and that the request was made; the app warns you about this beforehand. Otherwise the app sends no data over the internet.

Ads (Google AdMob). Ads appear only on the Check, Alert history and Settings pages; never in notifications, permission screens or alerts. Ads are requested as non-personalised and the app does not use the Advertising ID permission. To show an ad, Google's ad software processes data about the device and connection (IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device model and OS, ad interactions, diagnostics) under Google's privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). Your SMS, call, link, QR, app-list and alert-history data is never given to the ad software. You can turn ads off in the app with a one-time payment; once off, the ad software is not started. Payment is handled by Google Play / the App Store; we never receive your payment details.

5. Legal basis

Permissions and consents (notification access, call screening, camera, app scan) are based on your explicit consent (KVKK Art. 5/1) and can be withdrawn at any time in phone settings or in the app.

6. Transfers

Data processed on your device (§3) is not transferred to any third party, in Türkiye or abroad. While ads are on, the ad data listed in §4 is processed by Google, whose servers may be located abroad.

7. Retention and deletion

Alert history is deleted automatically after 30 days or immediately via Alert history → Clear history. App scan consent can be withdrawn in Settings → App scan. Uninstalling the app deletes all its data.

8. Your rights

Under Article 11 of KVKK you may request information about, correction or deletion of your personal data, object to processing and claim compensation. Since we do not hold your data, most requests are fulfilled by deleting data in the app or by uninstalling it. Contact: antiscam.destek@gmail.com (answered within 30 days).

9. Children

Reporting a number (optional)

If you tap "Report number" for a call in the alert history and confirm, the following is sent to our server: the phone number you report, the type (scam), your country calling code and a random identifier generated for this device. The identifier is not linked to your account, your phone number or your advertising ID. No message or call content, no contacts and not your own number are sent. The server stores the device identifier and your IP address only as irreversible hashes (to count how many different devices reported the same number and to limit abuse). Reports are deleted after 90 days. Numbers reported by enough different devices enter the list distributed to all users in that country. You can ask for a number to be removed from the list through our support address. The feature is off in builds where no server address is configured, and then nothing is sent.

Family protection (optional)

If you connect with a relative (the protecting person creates a code, the protected person enters it on their own phone and confirms), then when a warning appears on the protected phone only the type of the warning (for example "bank impersonation"), its verdict (suspicious / scam) and its channel (SMS, call, messaging app) are sent to our server and passed on to the protecting person's phone. No message text, sender, caller number or contacts are sent. The two phones are known to the server only by irreversible hashes of randomly generated device identifiers; no name, phone number or e-mail is requested. The names you give your relatives are stored only on your own phone. Event records are deleted after 7 days. Either side can disconnect at any time; the connection and its event records are then deleted from the server. If you never connect with anyone, nothing is sent for this feature.
