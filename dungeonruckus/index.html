Dungeon Ruckus

A fast dungeon brawler you can play together with a friend on the same Wi-Fi.

Support

Write to dungeonruckus.destek@gmail.com. Please add your player ID: open the game, tap the gear (SETTINGS) and read it under the buttons (for example "Player 8F3K-2LQ9").

Purchases

Bought something that did not arrive, or changed phones? SETTINGS > RESTORE PURCHASES brings back what the store has on record; if it does not, write to us with your player ID and the store's receipt.

For parents

The game is for players of all ages. It asks the player's age once and keeps the answer on the device. Under 16 it sends no play statistics. Children see only child-directed, non-personalised ads from Google AdMob. Purchases can be approved with Google Play's purchase approvals (Google Family Link). Details: Children.
